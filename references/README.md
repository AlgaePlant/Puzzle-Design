# References

Put the images your plan refers to here. List each one in the References part of `plan.md`, and say how it should be used.

- **Layout: match this.** A photo of a sketch drawn at phone size, a wireframe, or a screenshot. Say what to match: where things sit, their size, what is on each screen. Say what to leave: the paper, the pen lines.
- **Inspiration: the feel, not the thing.** An artwork, a precedent, or a photo. Name two or three qualities in your own words, such as the palette, the softness or the pace. Say what not to copy.
- **Code: start from this.** An example sketch. Copy the file here and say which part to take.

A few tips:

- Give files clear names, such as `layout-start-screen.jpg` or `mood-fog.jpg`.
- Use JPG or PNG. Most models cannot watch a GIF or a video. Use two or three stills, and describe the motion in words.
- Ask the agent to open the images. Some models skip images they are only told about.

The Project Demo asks for the sketches and mock-ups you worked from. Keep them here.
